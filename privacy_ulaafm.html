Ulaa FM Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

These documents are provided for transparency and compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Ulaa FM ("we", "our", or "us") provides radio streaming, podcast playback, and local music playback.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ulaa FM accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

2. Information We Access

Depending on features you use, Ulaa FM may access:

  • Audio and media files on your device (to browse and play local music)
  • Notification permission (to show media playback controls)
  • Network connectivity (to stream radio stations and podcasts)

Ulaa FM does not require account registration.

3. Personal Data Collection

At this time, Ulaa FM does not collect personal data directly from users, such as name, email address, phone number, or account credentials.

Ulaa FM does not provide in-app advertising and does not sell personal information.

4. How Information Is Used

  • To stream audio content requested by you
  • To enable local audio browsing and playback
  • To show playback notifications and controls
  • To maintain core app performance and functionality

5. Third-Party Services and Content Sources

Ulaa FM plays content provided by external radio and podcast sources. When you stream content, those content providers and their hosting infrastructure may process network-level information (for example, IP address and request metadata) as part of normal internet delivery.

Ulaa FM does not control how independent content hosts process data. Their own privacy terms may apply.

6. Data Storage and Retention

  • Playback preferences and app settings may be stored locally on your device.
  • Local media metadata is read from your device only for playback-related features.
  • Ulaa FM does not maintain a central user account database.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We do not share personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect app behavior and local data handling. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Ulaa FM is not specifically directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital age in your region).

10. Your Choices

  • Decline notification permission
  • Decline media access permission (local music features may not work)
  • Stop using the app at any time

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at the same public policy URL.
