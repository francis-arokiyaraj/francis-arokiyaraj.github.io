Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. It functions entirely on your device and does not send or receive data over the internet.

The app uses only basic sensor access (such as the magnetometer, accelerometer, or gyroscope) to display direction and level readings. These readings are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted or stored externally.
